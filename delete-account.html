Delete your account

ZoloTrip  ·  by Civorra  ·  Last updated: 9 October 2026

Two ways to delete

If you still have the app, deleting from inside it is instant. If you have already uninstalled ZoloTrip, email us and we will do it for you — you do not need to reinstall anything.

Option 1 — from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open ZoloTrip and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom.
  3. Scroll to the very bottom, to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete account (shown in red).
  5. Read the confirmation screen and confirm.

Your account is closed immediately and your data is removed as described below. You can then uninstall the app.

Option 2 — by email (no app needed)

Send an email from the same email address you used to sign in. We need that so we can be sure the request is really from you — we will not delete an account on the word of a third party.

Send to: rideonwhale@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my ZoloTrip account

In the message, include:

Please delete my ZoloTrip account and all my personal data.

Name on the account:
Email used to sign in:
City shown on my profile (helps us confirm):

I confirm this request is made by me, the account holder.

Open my email app

We confirm receipt within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. If we cannot verify that the request came from the account holder, we will write back and ask — we will not simply ignore you.

What we keep, and why

A small amount of information survives deletion. We are telling you plainly rather than burying it.

KeptWhyHow long
Reviews and ratings you gave to other travellers, with your name removed Other users made safety decisions based on them. Deleting them would quietly damage someone else's profile. Kept, anonymised
Reports filed about you, and any ban or removal record So that an account banned for harassment or a scam cannot simply come back under a new sign-in. Up to 3 years
Messages you sent that are still in another person's chat We cannot rewrite another person's conversation history. Your name is removed from them. Until that chat is deleted
Records we are legally required to retain Compliance with Indian law and any lawful request already received. As required by law
Just want to delete one thing? You do not have to close your whole account. In the app you can remove individual photos, clear your social username, delete a trip or a group, delete chat messages, and switch location off — all from Edit Profile and the relevant screen.

Before you delete

Deletion cannot be undone. We cannot restore a deleted account, its photos, its chats or its reviews. If you later want to use ZoloTrip again you will start fresh, with no rating and no history.
  • If you are mid-trip, tell your travel companions first — your chat will close and they will not be able to reach you through the app.
  • Save any photos you want to keep before you delete them.
  • Deleting your ZoloTrip account does not delete your Google account, and does not affect your Instagram, Facebook or other social accounts.

If you only want a break, you can simply sign out and leave the app installed. Nothing is deleted when you sign out.

Problems or complaints

If your deletion request is not actioned, or you are unhappy with how it was handled, contact our Grievance Officer:

grievance@civorra.com
