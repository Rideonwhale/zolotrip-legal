Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short

  • ZoloTrip helps you find travel companions. To do that we need your name, photo, a few profile details and your approximate location.
  • We never show your exact location to anyone. Map positions are deliberately shifted by roughly 300–600 metres before they leave your phone.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not run advertising networks inside the app.
  • You can delete your account and your data at any time, from inside the app or by email.

This page explains all of it in detail. If anything here is unclear, write to us and we will answer in plain language.

1. Who we are

ZoloTrip ("the app", "we", "us") is a travel companion matching app operated by Civorra, based in Uttar Pradesh, India. It is powered and managed by Rideonwhale.

This policy covers the ZoloTrip mobile app on Android and iOS, and these web pages. It applies to everyone who uses ZoloTrip, anywhere in the world.

For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Civorra is the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data Principal.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the core service — showing you trips, groups and travellers near you or heading where you are heading.
  • To let you communicate — delivering your chat messages to the people in your trip or group.
  • To help you judge who to travel with — showing profiles, ratings, reviews and whether someone has linked a social account.
  • To keep people safe — filtering abusive and obscene messages, handling reports, blocking and banning repeat offenders, and running SOS and trusted-contact alerts.
  • To meet legal obligations — responding to lawful requests and keeping records required by Indian law.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that have a legal effect on you.

4. Location — how it really works

This is the part people care about most, so here is exactly what happens.

a) Finding trips near you

When you open the "Near you" tab, the app reads your device's coordinates on your phone and compares them against the departure points that other users pinned when they posted their trips. Those pinned points were saved once, by them, at posting time. Nobody else's live location is involved, and nobody needs to have the app open for their trip to appear.

b) Blurring

Any position that is shown to another user is first shifted by a randomised offset of roughly 300 to 600 metres. The offset stays stable for a day so the marker does not jitter, and then changes. This means a map pin tells someone roughly which area you are in, never which building or street you are on.

c) "Raste mein" (live journey)

This optional feature lets you see other travellers who are on the road at the same time, heading the same way. It is off by default and you must switch it on yourself for each journey. While it is on:

  • The app writes your blurred position roughly every 10 minutes, together with your destination and travel mode.
  • It only does this while the app is open on your screen. Close the app and the updates stop.
  • Each update expires automatically after 45 minutes and is then ignored and removed.
  • You can switch it off at any time with the "Band karo" button, which also deletes your current position record immediately.
  • Your position is only matched against people within 50 km who are heading to roughly the same destination. Everyone else never sees it.

d) Sharing a location in chat

If you choose to send your location in a chat, that message goes to the people in that chat. This one is a deliberate action by you, so it is sent as you shared it.

You stay in control. ZoloTrip never asks for background location permission. If you deny or revoke location access, the app keeps working — you simply cannot use the distance-based features until you allow it again. You can change this at any time in your phone's settings.

7. How we protect it

  • All traffic between your phone and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Data is stored on Google Firebase, with access rules that limit each record to the users entitled to see it.
  • Location coordinates are blurred before they leave your device, so even our own database does not hold the precise position we show to others.
  • Photo metadata is stripped on upload.
  • Only the app administrators listed in section 12 can access the moderation panel.
An honest word on security. No app or internet service can promise perfect security. We use well-established tools and sensible defaults, but if a breach ever affects your personal data we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — and as a matter of our own policy for users everywhere — you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Correct anything inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Most of it you can edit yourself in Edit Profile.
  • Erase your data and close your account. See the deletion page.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including turning off location or removing your social username.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer, and if unresolved, escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

Write to grievance@civorra.com from your registered email address and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Age requirement

ZoloTrip is only for people aged 18 and over. The app does not accept an age below 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor is using ZoloTrip, please report the profile in the app or email us and we will remove the account.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes we will also show a notice inside the app. Continuing to use ZoloTrip after a change means you accept the updated policy.
