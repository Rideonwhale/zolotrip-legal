2. What ZoloTrip is — and is not

ZoloTrip is a platform that introduces travellers to each other. You can post a trip, discover trips and groups near you or along your route, request to join, chat, and travel together.

ZoloTrip is not a travel service. We do not organise trips, sell tickets, book hotels, provide transport, employ drivers or guides, handle payments, or accompany you anywhere. We are not a party to any arrangement you make with another user. Every trip, ride, booking, cost split and meeting is arranged directly between you and the other people involved, at your own risk.

Place names, map data and nearby listings (hotels, restaurants, cafes, ATMs, fuel, hospitals, attractions) come from OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Please verify anything important yourself.

To send join requests or create groups, the app requires a profile photo and a linked social account. For women-only trips, a photo alone is sufficient.

Group names are generated from the map location you pin, not typed freely, so that no one can use a group name to post something offensive.

5. Safety — your responsibility

We do not perform background checks, identity verification, criminal record checks or police verification on any user. A profile on ZoloTrip tells you what that person chose to tell you. It is not proof of who they are or that they are safe to travel with.

Please take these precautions. They are ordinary common sense, and they matter.

  • Video call or speak on the phone before agreeing to travel together.
  • Meet first in a public, busy place during daylight.
  • Add trusted contacts in the app, and tell a family member or friend your plan separately.
  • Read the person's reviews and ratings before committing.
  • Keep your own money, phone, charger and documents with you. Never hand over originals.
  • Trust your instinct. If something feels wrong, leave. You owe no one an explanation.
  • If you are in danger, contact local emergency services first — in India, dial 112. The app's SOS feature alerts your trusted contacts; it does not call the police for you.
  • Report and block anyone who behaves badly, so we can act and others are protected.

You travel at your own risk. You alone decide who to trust, where to go and what to agree to.

6. Social links and verification

Adding a social username helps other travellers see that you are a real person with a real presence. However:

  • We do not log in to the account, and typing a username does not prove you own it.
  • A linked social account is a signal of good faith, not a guarantee of identity or safety.
  • A verified badge, where shown, means only that an administrator checked something specific. It is still not a safety guarantee.

Please judge people on the whole picture — profile, photos, reviews, how they speak to you — and not on a single badge.

9. Rides, vehicles and money

If you offer or accept a ride through ZoloTrip:

  • It must be genuine cost-sharing between travellers, not a commercial taxi service. Running a paid transport business through ZoloTrip is not allowed.
  • The driver must hold a valid driving licence, valid insurance and valid vehicle documents, and must comply with all local traffic and transport laws.
  • Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • ZoloTrip does not verify any licence, insurance, permit or vehicle condition.
ZoloTrip handles no money. There is no payment, escrow, refund or guarantee inside the app. Any amount you pay or receive is entirely between you and the other person, and we cannot recover it for you. Be very careful with advance payments — that is the most common scam.

Accidents, breakdowns, delays, fines and disputes arising from a shared ride are the responsibility of the people involved.

10. Ratings and reviews

  • Only people who actually travelled together — that is, who shared a trip chat — can review each other.
  • One review per person per trip.
  • Reviews must be honest and based on your own experience. Do not post abuse, threats, private details or revenge reviews.
  • Reviews are visible to other users and remain on the profile, because other travellers rely on them for safety.
  • We may remove a review that breaks these terms, but we will not remove one simply because it is unflattering.

Vehicle ratings describe the vehicle and the driving, and are shown separately from a person's own rating.

11. Location features

The nearby and route-matching features need your approximate location. Positions shown to other users are deliberately blurred by roughly 300–600 metres, and the live "Raste mein" mode is off until you switch it on and stops when you close the app.

You may refuse or revoke location permission at any time; the app will still work, minus those features. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
