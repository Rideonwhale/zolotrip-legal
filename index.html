Where people look for companions

Most searches on ZoloTrip are for the trips people hesitate to make alone — Leh and Ladakh by bike, Manali and Kasol in the hills, Spiti in the summer window, Rishikesh and Haridwar over a long weekend, Kedarnath and Badrinath in yatra season, Goa and Gokarna off-season, Hampi, Jaipur and Udaipur on a short budget run, Varanasi, Ayodhya and Bodh Gaya for pilgrimage, Meghalaya and Shillong in the rains, Andaman for the water.

It is not an India-only app. Places come from OpenStreetMap, so any town on earth can be pinned — Kathmandu, Bangkok, Bali, Dubai, Tbilisi or your own district headquarters. If someone has posted a trip there, you'll find them.

Travelling with a stranger is not easy

So half the app exists for exactly this.

What the app does

  • Your exact location never leaves the phone. Every position is shifted 300–600 metres before anyone else sees it.
  • Abuse doesn't get sent. The message is blocked before delivery; repeat offenders are muted, then banned.
  • Group names come from a map pin, not a text box — so nobody can name a group something foul.
  • Ratings and reviews after each trip, written only by people who actually travelled together.
  • Trusted contacts and SOS — one tap alerts the people at home.
  • 18 and over only. Report and block are on every profile and every chat.

And what it does not do: we run no police verification and no background checks. A profile shows what a person chose to show. Meet in a crowded place the first time, tell someone at home where you're going, keep your own things with you — and if it feels wrong, leave. You owe nobody an explanation.

Questions people ask

What is ZoloTrip?

An Android app that introduces travellers heading the same way. You post where you're going and when; people going to the same place around the same dates show up, and you can chat and decide whether to travel together.

Does ZoloTrip book flights, trains, buses or hotels?

No, and it never will. Book your ticket wherever you normally do — IRCTC, the airline, a bus operator, a hotel site. ZoloTrip only helps you find the person to travel with. No money passes through the app at all.

Is it free?

Yes. Posting trips, searching, chatting and joining groups are all free. There is no subscription and no commission.

How do I find a travel partner for a specific place?

Search the destination, or post your own trip with that destination and your dates. Anyone whose trip overlaps with yours appears under "My way", wherever in the world they currently are.

Is it safe to travel with someone from an app?

It carries real risk, and we don't pretend otherwise. The app gives you ratings, reviews, reporting, blocking, blurred location, an abuse filter and SOS contacts. The rest is ordinary caution: video call first, meet in public, tell someone at home, and walk away the moment something feels off.

Can women use it safely?

There are women-only trips, which only women can request to join. Every profile can be reported and blocked, and messages are filtered before they are delivered. Women-only trips need only a photo, not a linked social account, so you share less to get started.

Which countries does it work in?

Anywhere. Place names come from OpenStreetMap, so any town in the world can be pinned. Most users today are in India.

Does the app track my location in the background?

No. It never asks for background location. Position updates happen only while the app is open on your screen, only if you switch on the live journey mode yourself, and each one expires after 45 minutes.
